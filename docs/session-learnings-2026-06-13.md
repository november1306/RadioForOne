## Interviewer (numbering continues from the 2026-06-10 file)

13. **Derive an engagement/demandingness meta-metric; never ask it directly.**
    The user designed this rule himself: "obviously you dont ask user directly
    'are you a meloman'? you derive that from walkaround context questions.
    still this categorization might be good to map initial direction for
    interviewer." Signals: answer length, vocabulary specificity, whether they
    give *reasons*. Use only as a steering prior for the opening minutes
    (drill depth, pace, radio-first vs discovery-first posture); the dossier
    overrides it as soon as real evidence lands. Caution: for two-tier
    listeners a scalar mispredicts in both directions — store per-slot if the
    user shows tiers ("hitting into specific 'complexity' is overfitting").
14. **Token tracks are a distinct dossier object.** When a user says a track
    is "not just a track", stop and elicit the story (what it points to, how
    it entered their life). Record as context-bound: poor similarity seed,
    high identity value, prime read-back/recognition material. A playlist
    carries pointers, not meanings — kindred-soul recognition is the ability
    to dereference them, and the dossier's job is making them dereferenceable
    by a stranger (or the curator).
15. **"How do you read other people through their taste?" is a high-yield
    domain-D probe.** The user's social matching criteria surface their own
    value axes projected outward (here: respect floor, complexity ceiling,
    articulacy as the match signal). Axes will differ per user — that's the
    point; the probe discovers which walls this person's house has.
16. **Self-initiated sessions: the user's topic IS the agenda.** A volunteered
    abstract topic (here: listener typologies) grounded back into their
    concrete examples carried an entire domain; the planned agenda (LP) waited
    and got richer for it.
17. **Demonstrated-over-stated has a low-status corollary; mine it.** "Music
    taste IS a reliable narrator" needs the caveat "the reliable narrator can
    still be quoted insincerely." The trustworthy signal is *unspoken,
    low-status* evidence (an unpopular deep cut sitting quietly in a favorites
    list), not the loud declaration ("man i love X so much") — the loud claim
    is often the pretender's tell. Curator/read-back consequence: surface a
    deep cut and it reads as recognition; name-drop the obvious token and it
    reads as flattery. Interviewer consequence: weight what a user
    *demonstrates* owning over what they *announce* loving, and treat an easy
    stylistic switch (an artist or a person shedding a style on command) as
    evidence of no fixed center — distinct from a stable artist wearing a
    theatrical stage-persona, which is not fakeness at all.

## Curator (numbering continues from the 2026-06-10 file)

8. **Never use token tracks as similarity seeds.** They are legal as
   recognition material — peaks whose why-line cites the story, not the sound
   ("from the same film-legacy shelf as..."). Seeding acoustic similarity from
   them optimizes the wrong dimension (the meme, not the meaning).
9. **A queue that is mostly familiar is a miss — it loses to the user's own
   shuffle.** Radio's value is being *themed but unpredictable*; serving known
   favorites back is no better than the shuffle button. Carry a per-user
   discovery floor (DM: ≥30% unfamiliar per queue) and satisfy it two ways,
   both legal: genuine discovery via the user's live lanes, AND **deep cuts /
   unfamiliar tracks of already-loved artists** — the low-risk way to hit the
   floor without a brand-new-artist bet. Prefer the *unpopular* known-artist
   track over the obvious one (demonstrated-over-stated → reads as recognition,
   not flattery; ties to lesson 17 and the token principle). Hard constraint:
   never fill the floor from the user's dead zone — that games the percentage
   while violating the exploration profile. Folded into `agents/curator.md`
   (rule 8b) 2026-06-13 under a second explicit founder authorization; the
   matching L2 eval (assert queues meet the discovery floor) is now part of the
   backfill debt.

## Curator (continued — queue-7 feedback, same day)

10. **A mood-coherent queue is a miss for an eclectic-radio listener.** Hitting
    the discovery floor by count is not enough: if the whole queue (and especially
    all the discovery slots) sits in one mood, the user reads it as "all hitting
    one nerve." Variance is a positive queue requirement, not just a saturation
    guard — match the stylistic spread of the user's endorsed library. Tempo/energy
    is a first-class pacing axis alongside intensity and attention; a mostly-slow
    queue fails even with good slow tracks. A cross-cutting affect *color* (here:
    melancholic) is never the queue's spine. Folded into `agents/curator.md`
    rule 7 + rule 8b, 2026-06-13, live founder authorization ("update curator").
    Eval debt: an L2 assertion that queue variance matches the library and that
    discovery slots span idioms, not one zone.
11. **Far-field / out-of-category ≠ another culture's folk-ritual music.** A
    recurring curator misread: equating "exotic" with "novel" and reaching for
    ethno-folk (Mongolian, Anatolian, Occitan, Nordic-ritual) as the far-field
    lane. The original cross-idiom win (DakhaBrakha) succeeded on earned-release
    architecture + the listener's *own* heritage identity, not on world-music
    exoticism — and the ethno-folk reaching has produced only misses since.
    Far-field = genre-distance inside an idiom the user has affinity for, or a
    genuine off-axis surprise. Folded into `agents/curator.md` rule 8.
12. **The deep-cut lane is the reliable arm of the discovery floor.** Unpopular
    tracks by already-loved artists land consistently ("very good alternative
    track pick") and read as recognition (ties to lesson 17 / the token principle).
    When a queue must hit the floor without betting every slot on a brand-new
    artist, lean here — but keep the deep cuts spread across moods too (lesson 10).
